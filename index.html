Wim van Dam

Bio

I am a quantum computer scientist and my work focuses on designing and implementing quantum algorithms and benchmarks for near term, fault tolerant quantum computers. Since 2022, I am a Principal Researcher at Microsoft Quantum. Before that, I was Head Quantum Algorithms at QC Ware and a tenured professor in computer science and physics at the University of California, Santa Barbara.

My work on quantum information science started with an MSc in Mathematics and Computer Science at Radbout University, The Netherlands. Following that, I got a PhD in physics at the University of Oxford and a PhD in computer science at the University of Amsterdam. Between those degree and my start as an assistant professor at UCSB, I was a postdoc at Umesh Vazirani's group at UC Berkeley and at Ed Farhi's group at MIT.
