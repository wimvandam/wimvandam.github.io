Wim van Dam

Scientific Bio

I am a quantum computer scientist and my work focuses on designing and implementing quantum algorithms and benchmarks for near term, fault tolerant quantum computers. Since 2022, I am a Principal Researcher at Microsoft Quantum. Before that I was Head Quantum Algorithms at QC Ware and a tenured professor at the departments of computer science and physics at University of California, Santa Barbara (UCSB).
My work on quantum information science started with a masters in Mathematics and Computer Science at Radboud University in The Netherlands. Following that, I got a DPhil in physics from the University of Oxford and a PhD in computer science from the University of Amsterdam. Between those degrees and my start as an assistant professor at UCSB, I was a postdoc at Umesh Vazirani's group at UC Berkeley and at Ed Farhi's group at MIT.

My Name

I'm originally from The Netherlands, and hence the following Dutch rules apply to my name:
  • The two spaces before and after the "van" are non-negiotable.
  • Alphabetically, my name is listed under the "D". Sorry co-authors.
  • The tussenvoegsel/particle "van" is only capitalized when it isn't preceded by my first name or my initial. Hence it's "W. van Dam", but "Dr. Van Dam".
  • The "W" in my first name is pronounced as the voiced labiodental approximant, which does not get used in common English. Those unfamiliar with this sound might think that they hear a "v" instead.
